# Bay — messaging & positioning

Frozen launch copy (Phase 14). Prefer these blocks in README, social posts, and `/about`.

## 1-sentence

Local-first AI garage agent that learns how you take care of your vehicles.

## 1-paragraph

Bay is a terminal-first garage intelligence CLI. Add vehicles, diagnose issues, plan service, track what’s due, and teach it your taste — DIY level, parts quality, budget, and risk — with everything stored on your machine. No accounts, no cloud sync, no dashboard. Decision-support only; not a certified mechanic.

## 3 value props

1. **Learns your taste** from Accept / Reject / Edit — not a generic chatbot.
2. **Real garage workflows**: diagnose, service plans, due items, ownership reports, mock OBD.
3. **Local-first & private** — data stays under `~/.bay`.

## Safety (public)

Decision-support only — not a certified mechanic. Diagnoses are ranked hypotheses, never certainty. High-risk systems (brakes, steering, airbags, EV high-voltage) warrant professional inspection.

## Short social / GitHub description

Local-first AI garage agent that learns how you take care of your vehicles.
